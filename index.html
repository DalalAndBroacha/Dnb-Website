Our Services

  • Wealth Management & Financial Planning

    With a team of resourceful & highly qualified experts, this desk helps our High Networth Clients, Family Office as well as Corporates manage their money through customised financial solutions.

  • Portfolio Managment Services

    Our portfolio management service is designed keeping in mind the needs of our High Net Worth customers. We offer complete end-to- end solutions for customers who are looking at maximizing their returns through expert fund management.

  • NRI Services

    Our dedicated NRI desk addresses the various needs of our many NRI clients spread across the globe. We strive to provide comprehensive solutions through expert empirical advice and perspectives drawn from over five decades of customer service.

  • Equity Advisory

    Our retail business is the backbone of our company. We emphasise on building steady, long-term relationships and are currently servicing over 20,000 registered clients throughout India, with the help of pan-india branches and widespread associates.

  • Institutional Equity

    Since the past four decades, our Institutional Equity Desk has grown exponentially. Today, we are in business with every major financial institution, bank and mutual fund in India, along with a large number of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). Our IED comprises of two functions – sales and dealing. The Institutional Equity Desk coordinates with our research department to provide world-class services to our customers.

  • Fixed Income & Distribution

    The distribution desk mainly deals with distribution of third party investment products like Government & Corporate Bonds, Mutual Funds,IPO’s and other saving instruments. This desk in coordination with our wealth management team offers clients alternative products to help maximise returns.

  • DEPOSITORY SERVICES

    We offer clients smooth, seamless, and paperless settlement of all trades and transactions.

  • Equity Research

    Our Research caters to both Institutional and Retail Clients. We are an idea based research outfit and we follow a bottoms up approach. We have a team of six analysts, each specialising in different sectors.

    Our Corporate Profile

    Over the past five decades, since our establishment in 1961, Dalal & Broacha Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. has provided impeccable financial solutions and services to a wide spectrum of customers with varied needs, ranging from equities and mutual funds; to wealth management and depository services. Our value added services include distribution of Mutual Funds, IPOs, and Portfolio Management Services.

    Over these five decades, we have evolved from an execution-focused team to integrated wealth managers. Our priority has been to facilitate corporate growth, by expanding our service portfolio to cater to ever evolving investor needs. We are currently handling over 20,000 clients, spread over retail, NRIs, corporates, banks, mutual funds, and FIIs.

    We are members of the Bombay Stock Exchange and the National Stock Exchange in the Cash and F&O segments. We are also Depository Participants with CDSL.
