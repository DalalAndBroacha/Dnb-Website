New Margin Rules applicable in Cash Margin w.e.f. 01/09/2020

 

What Changes?

 
  • Margin Shortage Penalty for Buying and Selling of Securities in cash Segment will be Applicable from 01/09/2020
  • Upfront Margin required @20 SEBI % in Cash Segment payable in advance (Refer NSE Circular ref.no.45206; Circular ref. no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOP/CIR/P/2020/146 dt.31.7.2020)
  • Margin must be received upfront on or before T day by RTGS / NEFT / Online Payment / Cheque Payment
  • Any margin payment received by RTGS / NEFT after T day will be treated as margin shortage and penalty will be levied
  • Client’s shares lying in their Demat Account either with Dalal & Broacha CDSL or outside DP can be considered for the margin requirement provided the approved securities are pledged with Dalal & Broacha Pledge account in CDSL / NSDL – SHCIL towards Margin Money
  • In short, Client account should have either credit balance or pay upfront margin @20% in advance or pledge shares with Dalal & Broacha Pledge Account before execution of any trade
  • On Sell of shares also, margin is applicable @20% and payable in advance before execution of any trade either by having Credit Balance in the Dalal & Broacha Ledger or by Advance or Early Payin or by having pledge shares in Dalal & Broacha Pledge Account
  • If client buys and sell on same day, margin is applicable @20% on both buy & sell transaction. Sell transaction, if early pay-in done than there is no margin applicable but benefit of early pay-in is not allowed for buy transaction
  • To overcome upfront margin issue and smooth trading of Buying and Selling of shares, it is recommended to have long term approved securities lying in your demat account, pledge with the broker to avail the margin benefit
  • In case if the client wants Sell the pledged security, the same is allowed and pay-in will be done from broker's pledge account

 

Mechanism to pledge the security in margin account

 
  • Securities held in clients demat account with the broker DP will not be treated as margin
  • To pledge the shares through CDSL, Client will have to fill up the Margin Request Pledge Form and submit the same to DP. DP will execute the form and client will receive an OTP through SMS and Email to give the consent that he has pledged the request for margin and confirm by way of clicking on the link given in the email
  • To avail the benefit of margin, Client can submit the pledge request form. If you do not pledge, margin short reporting will be done by broker to the exchange(Before placing request for margin, ensure your email id & mobile number registered with the CDSL & also Client code(UCC) mapped in the UCC of exchange)
  • T2T and High Volatility shares will not be considered for margin

 

Mechanism to pledge the security in margin account

 
  • Client can place the deposit (lumpsum amount) with the broker to avail the benefit of margin.

 

Trading Exposure

 
  • Those Clients either having credit balance OR Clients who have already pledged the securities to the brokers pledge account will get 4 times exposure to trade in the live market. For e.g.
  • If there is a credit balance or has pledged securities worth Rs. 1,00,000/-, the client can buy / sell securities worth upto Rs. 4,00,000/- till the settlement of the transactions.

 

To pay Attention

 
  • Ensure Clients Demat account and Trading account has identical mobile number & Email ID registered in CDSL, BSE & NSE. If any changes, modification in demat account or exchange UCC database require at least two days to verify and updating time. On a last moment it will not get change/modify instantly
  • Broker will accept Margin Pledge Request Form and initiate, but ultimate onus is on client level. Because Client will get OTP and Email to verify and accept the request
  • If Client demat/trading account is dormant and inactive in Exchange/CDSL level, client has to reactivate the account by submitting Re-KYC process which takes at least 1-2 days. To suggest client, let them do at least one trade to save time and process of dormant account
  • Those clients who holds more than one demat account, kindly map your one demat account as default for pay-out compulsory
  • The link to pledge the shares is: https://www.cdslindia.com/MarginPledge/OTP.aspx

Margin Shortage Penalty Table

 
  • Shortfall upto Rs.1 Lac = 0.5% penalty on shortage amount of margin
  • Shortfall more than Rs.1 Lac = 1.0% penalty on shortage amount of margin
  • Continuous Shortage of Margin for 3 days = 5.0% penalty on shortage amount of margin from 4th days start
  • In a month, any 5 days Shortage Margin = 5.0% penalty on shortage amount of margin from 5th day start
